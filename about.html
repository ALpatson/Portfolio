About Me

I’m a Software Engineering Master’s student at JUNIA ISEN in Lille, working mainly with Python, Java, JavaScript and Linux/Bash. During my research internship at ISIA Lab (Université de Mons), I built a rule-based system that routes free-text requests to the right handler, and I learned to test and measure AI tools before trusting them. I also build and run real products, like a live e-commerce store, and I enjoy documenting my work clearly and explaining it to non-technical people.
